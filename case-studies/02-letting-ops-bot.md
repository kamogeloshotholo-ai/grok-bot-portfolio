# 02 · Letting ops bot

## Problem
A small residential letting business in Gauteng found landlords and tenants by hand on Facebook and handled every enquiry, viewing and deposit question on WhatsApp. Leads were missed and the paperwork piled up.

## What the bot does
- Scans local Facebook rental groups and Marketplace for landlord listings and people looking for rooms.
- Logs every lead in one sheet and asks the owner which ones to contact.
- Replies to rental enquiries on WhatsApp Business with approved templates.
- Books viewings and sends reminders.
- Drafts leases, deposit payment plans and a sole-mandate agreement for landlords.
- Coordinates move-outs, inspections and deposit refunds.

## How it's built
- Browser sessions on Facebook and WhatsApp Business Web, signed in by the owner.
- A Google Sheet as the pipeline (landlords, tenants, units, viewings).
- Message templates written by the bot and approved by the owner before use.
- Routines: a WhatsApp check every 15 minutes and scheduled Facebook scans.
- Rule: the bot finds and logs leads, and the owner decides who gets contacted.

## Result (Nov 2026)
- Joined 8 local rental groups during setup.
- Early in setup the sheet held 14 landlords and 2 tenant seekers.
- Landlord leads are numbered up to L036.
- Booked viewings, including one for 25 Nov 2026.
- Arranged a move-out inspection and deposit refund for 27 Nov 2026.
- No signed lease or placement fee is attributed to the bot yet.

## What's next
- Track leads through to signed leases and report the conversion rate.
- Put the sole-mandate agreement into use with landlords.
