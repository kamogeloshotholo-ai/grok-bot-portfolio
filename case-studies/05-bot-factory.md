# 05 · Bot factory

## Problem
"Build me a bot" requests are usually vague. Bots built from vague requests try to do everything and do none of it well.

## What the bot does
- Interviews the owner for the goal, the process and the judgment calls.
- Writes a bot with one job, clear rules and a list of things it must never do.
- Creates the bot and gives it its own schedule.

## How it's built
- Grok Bot's bot-creation tools.
- A design checklist: one job, one voice, explicit anti-jobs, no leftover tools.
- New bots receive their routines through a handoff message and confirm their connectors work.

## Result (19–23 Nov 2026)
- Built Freebie Hunter, Competition Entrant (case study 04), Gold Options Trader and Portfolio Builder, along with others that are not shown here.
- An earlier version built Contest Entry (case study 06) and a research bot.

## What's next
