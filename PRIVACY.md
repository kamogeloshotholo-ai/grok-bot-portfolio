# Privacy

These case studies come from real work, so they were cleaned before publishing.

**Removed everywhere**
- Names of tenants, landlords, customers, contest posters and any other third party
- Addresses, phone numbers, ID numbers and email addresses
- Logins, API keys, tokens and passwords
- Account balances, bank details, tax and company registration numbers
- Family details
- Trading results of any kind

**Kept**
- Counts and dates taken directly from the bots' logs
- Public on-chain data for the Agent Job Market contract, and its public site

**Generalised**
- The letting and tender case studies describe the business and the problems in general terms, not the specific companies or people involved.
